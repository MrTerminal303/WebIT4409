See the club in motion, on paper, and on the map.

Every month the club gathers to print, photograph, film and argue happily about all three. This page collects the best of it in one place: photographs shown as large as they deserve to be, a short film of the year so far, and a map that drops you at our meeting place.

In the frame this month

One large print hangs beside the door all season, and we swap it every few months so there is always a fresh face — or thirty — to greet you when you arrive. Bring a camera next time: the next picture on this wall includes you.

Workshops run from noon until the ink runs out. Beginners are shown the press in the first hour; everyone leaves with something printed, if not always perfectly.

Watch the club year in two minutes

Filmed by members at meetups, workshops and the summer exhibition, with the sound left exactly as it was on the day. Press play for a quick tour of where we have been this year — and what we managed to make along the way.

Find the club

We meet on alternate Saturdays, just inside the park — look for the table covered in paper. If the map does not load in your browser, open the same location on OpenStreetMap instead.
